# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Students and independent learners who already work in a terminal and want guided study without moving into a browser. Their main task is to start or resume a study session, attach useful material, and work through one study mode at a time with the keyboard.

## Product purpose

OpenStudy is a local-first terminal study assistant. It combines a learner's subject, chosen model, reasoning level, source material, and language into focused study modes. Success means a learner can open the CLI, provide minimal direction, and begin useful study work without managing a general-purpose AI agent.

## Brand personality

Calm, focused, and direct. OpenStudy should feel like a quiet study desk. The interface explains actionable states clearly and otherwise stays out of the way.

## Anti-references

OpenStudy should not resemble a web dashboard squeezed into a terminal, a generic chat transcript, an analytics product, or an agent control panel. Avoid dense chrome, decorative color, fake activity, hidden controls, and modal-heavy workflows.

## Design principles

- Keep the prompt and active study task visually dominant.
- Preserve fast, discoverable keyboard operation.
- Show only context that helps the learner act or understand the current state.
- Remove secondary UI before compressing primary content on small terminals.
- Build study modes around learning behavior, not generic chat conventions.

## Accessibility & inclusion

Every major interaction must work without a mouse. Do not rely on color alone for selection or status. Keep important text legible against the black background, respect the minimum terminal size, and use clear labels for loading, errors, focus, and current state. Avoid motion unless it represents real work.
